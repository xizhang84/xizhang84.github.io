Research Themes

I design the detectors that decide what a PET or SPECT scanner can see: the crystal that turns a gamma ray into light, the scanner it sits in, and the electronics that read it out, treated as one system rather than separate parts. Four threads, each with the papers behind it.

Laser writes barriers → γ absorbed → light stays in its pixel. Move the pointer to aim.

Laser-Processed Converging-Pixel CsI:Tl Detectors

A femtosecond laser writes optical barriers inside an intact 50 × 50 × 8 mm CsI:Tl crystal, turning it into a 25 × 25 array with no sawing, no reflectors and 100% process yield. The pixels converge from 1.6 mm at the entrance face to 2 mm at the photodetector, so each pixel axis follows the collimator’s line of response and depth-of-interaction parallax disappears without any DOI decoding. A four-axis pencil-beam platform calibrates the detector, and maximum-likelihood decoding resolves every pixel where center-of-gravity fails: 1.00 ± 0.42 mm localization accuracy and 11.8% energy resolution at 122 keV.

  • Maximum-likelihood position decoding of laser-processed converging-pixel CsI:Tl detectors for high-resolution SPECT · IEEE TRPMS, under review
  • Laser-processed CsI:Tl detector with converging pixels · IEEE NSS/MIC 2025
  • Grooved microstructures on LYSO with a femtosecond laser · Med Phys 2025
  • Femtosecond-laser processing of photonic crystals · IEEE NSS/MIC 2020

DC-SPECT: A Cardiac-Dedicated SPECT Scanner

Dynamic Cardiac SPECT is a static, body-contouring scanner with about 230° of detector coverage: 80 laser-processed CsI:Tl modules, each behind its own tungsten loft-hole collimator, built for diagnostic and theranostic cardiac imaging. I build and calibrate the modules and produced the scanner’s first experimental images. With list-mode MLEM reconstruction, a 57Co point source and a 99mTc triple-line phantom were resolved with FWHM down to 3.55 mm (neural-network decoder) and 4.04 mm (maximum likelihood), comparing MLE and deep-network event positioning under real scanner conditions. The same detector platform serves an NIH-funded dynamic brain SPECT.

  • First experimental and phantom results of the DC-SPECT scanner · IEEE NSS/MIC 2026, submitted
  • Enhancing detector event positioning with L1 regularization · IEEE NSS/MIC 2025
  • Modular and robust PET platform for evaluating detectors · IEEE NSS/MIC 2025

Monolithic-Ring PET Detectors

A preclinical PET detector cut from a single hollow LYSO cylinder (48.5 mm bore, 5 mm wall, 25.1 mm long) with two rings of 46 SiPMs air-coupled to its ends: no pixels, no reflectors, a 100% fill factor and no edges anywhere around the ring. Center-of-gravity and neural-network decoding of the light distribution locate each gamma to 0.94 mm FWHM circumferentially and 2.45 mm axially, with 10.7% energy resolution at 511 keV; a Derenzo phantom resolves 1.2 mm rods. The same ring became a portable gamma sensor, and stacks of sliced rings a full scanner concept.

  • A preclinical PET detector constructed with a monolithic scintillator ring · Phys Med Biol 2019
  • Preclinical PET scanner from stacks of sliced monolithic rings · IEEE NSS/MIC 2019
  • Portable gamma sensor from a monolithic LYSO ring · Sensors 2021
  • Two readout methods for a monolithic scintillator ring PET · IEEE NSS/MIC 2020

SiPM Readout Electronics

Sigma-delta modulation turns each SiPM channel into a 1-bit stream counted in an FPGA, so a 64-channel front end runs from a single 5 V supply at about 3 W with no external ADCs. My configurable dual-CDL design adds two switchable discharge lines: a dual-polarity mode that reads either SiPM polarity in software, and a single-polarity mode with fast baseline restoration and precise charge zeroing for 80–364 keV SPECT signals. On a 50 × 50 × 8 mm CsI:Tl crystal it reaches 7.88% energy resolution at 122 keV against 13.9% for a commercial CAEN V1740 digitizer, with a 63% higher event rate and cleaner pixel separation on the laser-processed array. Earlier work: a 100-channel readout system and an 8.8 ps RMS FPGA time-to-digital converter.

  • A configurable dual-CDL sigma-delta readout circuit for CsI:Tl SPECT · IEEE NSS/MIC 2026, submitted
  • An advanced 100-channel readout system for nuclear imaging · IEEE TIM 2019
  • 8.8 ps RMS FPGA time-to-digital converter · Sensors 2020
  • Compensating time-walk errors in PET timing · IEEE TRPMS 2020
